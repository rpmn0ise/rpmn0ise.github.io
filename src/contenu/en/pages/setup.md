Here is what I use every day, with what I think of it when I have an opinion. Page updated on 8 November 2026.

## PC and desk

- **Processor**: AMD Ryzen 5 7600X
- **Motherboard**: MSI B650 Gaming Plus WiFi
- **Memory**: 2 × 16 GB DDR5-5600
- **Graphics card**: MSI RTX 4060 Ventus 2X Black OC
- **Storage**: 2 TB Samsung 990 Pro SSD, 1 TB MSI M450 SSD, 4 TB hard drive (ST4000LM024)
- **Case**: MAG Forge 320R Airflow
- **System**: Windows 11 Pro
- **Monitors**: VG279QL1A as the main one, VY279HGR as the second
- **Keyboard**: Royal Kludge RK M87
- **Mouse**: Logitech M305
- **Mouse pad**: Corsair
- **Microphone**: Tonor, for voice

## Music production

I don't have any studio gear: I produce with my headphones and the desk's audio system, and I use nothing else.

- **Software**: FL Studio, Producer edition
- **Plugins**: Serum 2, FabFilter, and a few others
- **Headphones**: Soundcore Life Q30
- **Speakers**: Samsung HT-Z120 home cinema system, repurposed
- **Sounds**: no sample packs, I make everything myself, or I try to
- **No MIDI keyboard or pads**

I mix on headphones. To find out whether the result holds up, I ask other people for their opinion and I test on other speakers.
