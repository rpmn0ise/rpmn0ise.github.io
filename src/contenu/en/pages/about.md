I'm rpmn0ise. I build projects around the things that interest me, without sticking to a single field. Web development, car simulation, hard music, computing: I move from one to the other depending on the ideas that come to me and the problems I feel like solving.

I'm developing [Retour de Force](/en/projects/retour-de-force/), a mod catalogue for BeamNG.drive. I run the site and the Discord community on my own. I handle access to the catalogue, the checks and the selection of mods. I also created [Hard Music FR](/en/projects/hard-music-fr/), a French-speaking Discord community dedicated to hard music, where I try to introduce people to artists and bring listeners together.

On the side, I'm building [Interligne](/en/projects/interligne/), a French-language fanzine where I pick subjects that catch my attention, then check the texts, their sources and how reliable they are. I'm also learning to produce hard music with FL Studio, working on kicks and arrangements in particular. Web development lets me give a concrete form to several of these ideas.

My way of working is mostly about searching, testing and adjusting. I can start with a technical detail and end up building a tool around the problem. This site gathers a selection of my projects and gives an overview of what I do, following my interests as they go.
