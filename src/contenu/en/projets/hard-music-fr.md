Hard Music FR is a French-speaking Discord community dedicated to hard music in all its forms: hardstyle, rawstyle, hard trance, uptempo, frenchcore, speedcore, terrorcore, hardcore, gabber, doomcore, hard techno, schranz, EBM/industrial, slowtempo, breakcore, psycore and freeform.

I created it in early August 2026. I take care of the server's structure, moderation and welcoming new members on my own.

## Why

There was no general-purpose, lively French-speaking community for this scene. The existing servers are either international and English-speaking, or centred on sharing tracks and promotion, without any real community life.

I wanted a place where French-speaking fans share their discoveries, talk about the scene and meet up in voice chat to listen together, without any imposed ritual.

## What I do there

- I welcome each new member individually, rather than with an automatic message.
- I moderate the server on my own.
- I wrote the rules and the FAQ, and I keep evolving them with the server.
- I reach out to emerging French-speaking artists, being honest about the server's current size. One artist joined after a personal message.
- I run the channels to keep things active (recurring formats, resources, sharing productions).

## How it works

The server is organised into five categories:

- **Welcome**: welcome, rules, role selection, announcements, FAQ
- **Community**: general, introductions, bangers-of-the-day, scene-and-news, studio, resources, events-and-festivals, off-topic
- **Voice**: a chill channel and a listening and reactions channel
- **Support**: suggestions and tickets
- **Staff**: moderation area

A few details about how it works:

- Reaction roles let you pick your favourite styles.
- A "voice alert" role can be mentioned by anyone to let people know a session is starting.
- An "Artist" role identifies the producers and artists on the server.
- `#studio` hosts members' productions, works in progress and remixes.
- `#resources` is a forum of production resources (YouTube channels, plugins, tutorials), sorted by tags.
- The server is open from age 16, with a short set of rules and progressive sanctions.

## Where the project stands

Growth is deliberately gradual. I'd rather welcome people one by one than aim for a peak in members that nobody welcomes. Part of the members are active every day, and the rest drop by more occasionally.
