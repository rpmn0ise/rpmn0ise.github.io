I've been making hard music for less than three months. My studio is simple: a PC, FL Studio (Producer edition), Soundcore Life Q30 headphones and a repurposed Samsung home cinema system. No studio gear, and I don't really feel like buying any for now.

I chose FL Studio on a recommendation, and I've been using it since the start.

## Mix on headphones, then check elsewhere

I produce and mix on headphones. The problem is that headphones don't tell you everything: every listening system colours the sound in its own way, and what sounds good on headphones can sound different on speakers or on a phone. So once a track is finished, I listen to it on other devices before forming an opinion.

## The best quality control: my friends

I also have my friends listen to my tracks, friends who listen to hard music too. Their opinion counts, because they know the style.

Their feedback taught me one thing: I don't always master my tracks very well. Mixing is about balancing the sounds against each other (the kick, the bass, the synths, the vocals). Mastering is the finishing step: you set the overall level and the coherence of the track so that it holds up everywhere. Both take work, and I still have a way to go on the second.

## And what's next?

No shopping list. For now, I'd rather learn with what I have, listen on several systems and listen to my friends. If the gear ends up limiting me, I'll know.
