I rebuilt this site from scratch. The structure of the old one no longer suited me, and its complexity made every addition heavier than it needed to be.

## What I put in it

A selection of projects with their descriptions, an About page, and this journal, where I write when I have something to say. It's not a corporate portfolio: it's an overview of what I do, following my interests as they go.

## How it's built

These are static pages generated with Eleventy from simple Markdown files, designed for Cloudflare Pages. The fonts are embedded in the site, no external service is needed, and adding an article just means creating a file.
