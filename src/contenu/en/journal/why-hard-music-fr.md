Hard Music FR is a French-speaking Discord community dedicated to hard music. I created it in early August 2026. The [project page](/en/projects/hard-music-fr/) describes what it is and how it works. Here, I mostly talk about why I launched it and what I take from it.

## The starting idea

I wanted a real community, general-purpose, open to all hard music: hardstyle, frenchcore, hardcore, gabber, hard techno and the rest. The servers I saw were either English-speaking, or centred on sharing tracks and promotion, without any real shared life. There was no French-speaking place where people talk to each other, share their discoveries and discuss the scene.

## The hardest part: the first members

At the beginning, the hardest thing is handling the first members and getting people to come. A server that's just starting is mostly a room where you have to make people want to stay.

## What I learned

I learned to welcome new members better. I welcome them one by one, rather than with an automatic message. It takes longer, but I think it matters.

## What I'd do differently

If I started over, I'd build a better structure right from the start, and I'd create the website at the same time as the server.

## What's next

I hope the community will be livelier and that it will thrive. I have no secret plan: I keep welcoming, running things and adjusting.

If you listen to hard music and you speak French, you can [join the Discord](https://discord.gg/AQBuVjqNxs).
