My car audio project hasn't started yet. I haven't bought any gear: for now, I'm doing my research and saving up.

## The goal

I want a balanced sound, with good bass. Not just "it hits hard": I want music to sound the way it's supposed to sound. Music is still music, whether you listen to it on headphones, on speakers or anywhere else, and I want it to be well served.

## What I need to understand

Car audio isn't just about buying a big subwoofer. There are several subjects to understand, and that's what keeps me busy right now:

- **The vocabulary and how it works**: subwoofer, amplifier, speakers, filters, gain setting. It's not a volume knob.
- **The wiring**: cable gauge, power supply, protection. This is the part where a mistake costs a lot.
- **The parts**: knowing which component depends on which other one. An amp, a sub and speakers have to be chosen together.
- **The prices**: understanding what each part costs, so I don't spend in the wrong place.

## My plan

My idea is to buy everything, but not before I've prepared a detailed plan. The plan first, the gear after: that way I avoid buying twice what doesn't go together.

I'll tell the rest here when there is a rest.
