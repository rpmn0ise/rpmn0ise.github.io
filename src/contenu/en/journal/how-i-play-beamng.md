I play a lot of things: Counter-Strike 2, Assetto Corsa, plenty of small indie games. But let's be honest, it's always BeamNG.drive that comes back.

What I like about BeamNG is that it doesn't force a single way of playing on me. I go realistic when I want to drive cleanly, cruising when I feel like driving with no destination, drifting when I feel like making noise with the tyres, and derby when I feel like smashing everything. I switch from one to another, without limiting myself to a single one.

To drive, I have a Logitech G29 wheel. I'm also planning to buy a new controller to replace the old one.

BeamNG is also behind one of my projects: [Retour de Force](/en/projects/retour-de-force/), a mod catalogue for the game, with its own Discord community.
