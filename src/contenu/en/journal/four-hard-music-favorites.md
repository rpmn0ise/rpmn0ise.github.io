Four hard music tracks I love, with what I find in them.

- **Le Bruit, RDØ**: I just love it. It's French, and the kicks are properly heavy, in slowtempo the way we like it.
- **Together, Hysta and RDØ**: two of my favourite artists on the same track. Hardcore kicks, then an uptempo build-up by RDØ. Pure joy.
- **Belong to One, Hysta**: a properly hardcore track. Hysta is the best.
- **Distorshøn, Antenora, RDØ and KRUELLA**: simply too good.

My selection is also on the [Music](/en/music/) page, along with what I'm listening to right now.
