SGPI Community

SGPI is a French tech and cyber community focused on knowledge sharing, experimentation, and digital culture.

What is SGPI?

SGPI brings together people interested in technology, cybersecurity, open source software, operating systems, and digital experimentation. It is a place to learn, exchange, and build skills.

Community Philosophy

  • Curiosity and learning first
  • Respect and responsibility
  • Technical discussions over hype
  • Open-minded experimentation

My Role

I am the founder of SGPI. This project reflects my interest in technology, cybersecurity, and structured learning through community-driven exchange.

Access & Orientation

SGPI is not designed for mass audiences. Access is oriented toward people genuinely interested in tech, cyber, and open source culture.
