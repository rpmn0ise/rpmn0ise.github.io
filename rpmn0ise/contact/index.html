Contact

This page exists for real contact, not noise.

If you’re here to report an issue, ask something specific, or reach me for a legitimate reason, you’re in the right place. If you’re here to send vague messages, promotions, or empty requests, this page will save us both some time.

How to reach me

I don’t use contact forms or public email addresses. I prefer platforms where context, history, and intent are visible.

  • Discord — Primary hub (community, tech, gaming, discussions)
    Best option for access to the SGPI community.
    My discord username : rpmn0ise
    → SGPI gateway page
  • GitHub — Projects, code, experiments
    Best for technical feedback, issues, or collaboration.
    GitHub Profile
  • Reddit — Discussions, exploration, digital culture
    Similar spirit to Discord, but more asynchronous.
    Reddit Profile
  • Instagram — Inspiration & passive presence
    Not a contact channel. Messages are likely ignored.
  • YouTube — Learning & entertainment
    Comments are fine. DMs are not expected.

What you can contact me for

  • Reporting errors or broken content on this site
  • Specific and well-framed questions
  • Personal contact with context
  • Long technical discussions
  • Collaboration or project-related topics

What I don’t respond to

  • Vague requests without context
  • Unlimited free support
  • Spam, promotions, or self-advertising
  • Repeated messages for attention

Silence is not personal. It’s a filter.

Response expectations

  • I reply when I have time
  • No guarantee of response
  • Clear, structured messages have a higher chance of being answered

Clear intent > urgency.

Language

English and French only.

Community note

If your goal is discussion, learning, or long-term exchange, the SGPI community is the right place — not private messages.

The contact page is a door.
The community is the room.
